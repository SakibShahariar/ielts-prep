# IELTS Writing Task 1 — Linkers, Connectors and Synonyms

**Quick reference for Band 8–9.** Sectioned by where to use each item, plus adjective/adverb
synonym tables. Use for Task 1; many items also carry over to Task 2.

> ⚠️ **Golden rule for Band 8–9:** linkers should be **almost invisible**. A few precise ones
> plus good referencing (`this trend`, `the former`, `such a rise`, `the figure`) beat a long
> mechanical chain of `Firstly → Secondly → In conclusion`.

---

# PART 1 — LINKERS & CONNECTORS BY LOCATION

## 1. Introduction — naming the visuals and linking them

| Item | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **The provided [chart/table/map/diagram] illustrates...** | Standard opening | The provided bar chart illustrates information regarding... |
| **while** | Link two visuals (neutral) | The table shows funding sources, **while** the pie charts display spending. |
| **whereas** | Link two visuals (contrast) | The table gives figures in £m, **whereas** the charts show percentages. |
| **regarding / concerning / about** | What the visual is about | information regarding the sales of... |
| **over a period of X years, from Y to Z** | Time span | over a period of six years, from 1995 to 2000 |
| **between X and Y** | Time span | between 1985 and 2015 |
| **with projections for 2030 and 2040** | Future data | ...with projections for 2030 and 2040. |
| **in a particular year (2007)** | Single year | ...the sales of X in a particular year, 2007. |
| **figures are given in millions of pounds (£m)** | Units | Funding figures are given in £m, and expenditure is shown as a percentage. |

## 2. Overview — the big picture

| Item | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Overall, it is evident that...** | Standard overview opener | Overall, it is evident that sales fluctuated. |
| **In summary, ...** | Alternative | In summary, all four categories increased. |
| **Looking at the broad picture, ...** | Alternative | Looking at the broad picture, Malaysia remained highest. |
| **Broadly speaking, ...** | Generalisation | Broadly speaking, the trend was upward. |
| **The most striking feature is that...** | Highlight one key point | The most striking feature is that X overtook Y. |
| **while / whereas** | Contrast two series in the overview | X rose steadily, **whereas** Y fluctuated. |
| **Another interesting point is that...** | Second overview point | Another interesting point is that X was the most popular at the end. |
| **In addition / Moreover** | Add a second point | In addition, the airport was renamed. |

⚠️ Overview = **no exact figures**. One clear frame + one contrast is enough.

## 3. Body — sequencing a PROCESS

| Stage | Linkers | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Beginning** | first · firstly · at first · initially · to begin with · at the beginning (of the process) · in the initial stage · commencing/starting/beginning with · the first step is | At the first stage, flour is delivered by truck. |
| **Middle** | then · next · after that · subsequently · afterwards · following this · in the following step · in the subsequent stage · once · before · prior to · while | The sheets are **subsequently** cut into strips. |
| **End** | finally · lastly · last · eventually · in the final stage · culminating in · ending with · the last step is | ...**culminating in** labelling and sealing. |

⚠️ Do not use `firstly → secondly → thirdly` for all eight stages. Mix `then`, `next`,
`subsequently`, `in the following stage`. **step = stage**.

## 4. Body — describing MAP changes

| Use | Linkers / verbs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Add a change | in addition · moreover · similarly · in the same way · at the same time · meanwhile | In addition, a housing estate was built. |
| Contrast changes | while · whereas · by contrast · however | The international terminal expanded, **while** the domestic one remained unchanged. |
| Something new | was constructed · was built · was added · was introduced · was established | A new runway **was constructed**. |
| Something removed | was demolished · was removed · was replaced by | The factories **were demolished**. |
| Name/position change | was renamed · was relocated · was repositioned · was moved | The airport **was renamed** Tilden International. |
| Size change | was expanded · was enlarged · was extended · was reduced · shrank | The terminal **was expanded**. |

## 5. Comparison & contrast (all chart types)

| Item | Use | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **whereas / while** | Direct contrast | Most useful in Task 1 overviews |
| **by contrast / in contrast** | Stronger contrast | Often starts a sentence |
| **conversely** | Opposite situation | Formal; use sparingly |
| **on the other hand** | Second side / second visual | Your teacher's body-2 opener |
| **compared with / compared to** | Comparison | `compared with` = difference; `compared to` = similarity |
| **in comparison with** | Formal comparison | Good for data |
| **unlike** | Difference | *Unlike X, Y increased.* |
| **similarly / likewise / in the same way** | Similarity | Good for maps |
| **however / nevertheless** | Concession | Punctuation: `...; however, ...` |

## 6. Cause & result (mainly Task 2, but useful)

| Cause | Result |
|---|---|
| because · as · since · owing to · due to · given that · for this reason | therefore · thus · hence · consequently · as a result · accordingly |

⚠️ `because/as/since` + clause; `due to/owing to` + noun. Mind punctuation: *Therefore, ...*

## 7. Concession & exception

| Item | Example |
|---|---|
| although · though · even though | Although it was the smallest, it grew fastest. |
| despite · in spite of | Despite the fall, the figure recovered. |
| admittedly · granted that | Admittedly, the early years were flat. |
| except for · apart from | Apart from Malaysia, all four rose. |
| while it is true that... | While it is true that X fell, Y rose. |

## 8. Approximation & data phrasing (not linkers, but essential)

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| approximately · roughly · about · around | Approximate a figure |
| close to · just under · just over · nearly · almost | Near a value |
| in the region of · in excess of | Formal approximation |
| at its peak · at its lowest point · reaching a high of | Extremes |
| respectively | Match two lists in order |
| by contrast, ... | Difference in figures |

## 9. Closing a paragraph

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| marking the final step of the process | Process |
| completing the packaging stage | Process |
| in the last two years it remained stable at... | Chart/map |
| after which sales levelled off | Chart |
| reaching just under 50% | Chart |

⚠️ Task 1 has an **Overview**, not a "Conclusion". Avoid `In conclusion` — that's Task 2.

## 10. Overused linkers — replace these

| Avoid | Better |
|---|---|
| Firstly / Secondly / Thirdly everywhere | then · next · subsequently · in the following stage |
| And / But / So | whereas · however · therefore |
| Also | moreover · in addition · equally (sparingly) |
| In conclusion (Task 1) | Overall / In summary |
| a lot of linkers in one paragraph | referencing: `this trend`, `the former`, `such a rise`, `the figure` |

# PART 2 — ADJECTIVES & ADVERBS WITH SYNONYMS

## 1. Degree of change (Task 1 core)

| Degree | Adjective | Synonyms | Adverb | Synonyms | Safe collocation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **High** | significant | considerable · substantial · marked · notable · major | significantly | considerably · substantially · markedly · notably | a significant increase / increased significantly |
| **High** | dramatic | striking · sharp · steep | dramatically | sharply · steeply · strikingly | a dramatic rise / rose dramatically |
| **High** | remarkable | striking · impressive · extraordinary | remarkably | strikingly · impressively | a remarkable recovery |
| **High** | drastic | severe · radical · extreme | drastically | severely · radically | a drastic fall |
| **High** | substantial | considerable · significant · large | substantially | considerably · significantly | a substantial increase |
| **High** | marked | clear · noticeable · pronounced | markedly | clearly · noticeably | a marked contrast |
| **Medium** | moderate | modest · steady · measured | moderately | modestly · steadily | a moderate increase |
| **Medium** | steady | consistent · gradual · stable | steadily | consistently · gradually | a steady rise / rose steadily |
| **Low** | slight | minor · marginal · small | slightly | marginally · minimally | a slight decline / declined slightly |
| **Low** | marginal | minor · slight · negligible | marginally | slightly · barely | a marginal change |
| **Low** | minimal | negligible · tiny | minimally | barely · hardly | a minimal increase |
| **No change** | stable | steady · constant · unchanged · static | — | remained stable / unchanged / static | remained stable at 40% |

⚠️ `equipment` is uncountable — never `equipments`. `steady` is an adjective; use `steadily` as the adverb.

## 2. Direction words

| Direction | Adjectives | Adverbs / verbs |
|---|---|---|
| Up | upward · rising · increasing · growing | rose · climbed · surged · soared · escalated |
| Down | downward · falling · declining · decreasing | fell · declined · dropped · plummeted · slumped |
| Flat | flat · level · static · unchanged | levelled off · remained constant · plateaued |
| Irregular | fluctuating · volatile · uneven | fluctuated · oscillated |

## 3. Popular general adjectives & adverbs (with synonyms)

| Headword | Adjective synonyms | Adverb | Adverb synonyms | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| important | crucial · vital · essential · key · significant | importantly | crucially · vitally · essentially | `significant` also means "noticeable" |
| good | beneficial · advantageous · favourable · sound · effective | well | effectively · successfully | `good` is vague — pick the sense you mean |
| bad | detrimental · adverse · harmful · poor · inadequate | badly | severely · poorly | `bad influence` → `detrimental influence` |
| big | major · substantial · considerable · enormous | — | greatly · considerably | `large` = neutral/formal; `huge` too informal for data |
| small | minor · marginal · modest · limited | — | slightly · marginally | `small` adjective; `slightly` adverb |
| clear | evident · apparent · obvious · unmistakable | clearly | evidently · obviously | `apparently` = "seeming", not "clearly" |
| likely | probable · possible · potential | likely | probably · possibly · potentially | `It is likely that...` |
| different | distinct · varied · differing · contrasting | differently | distinctly · markedly | `different than` → `different from` |
| similar | comparable · alike · similar | similarly | likewise · comparably | `similar to` |
| high | elevated · substantial · considerable | highly | greatly · considerably | `highly likely` but not `highly big` |
| low | minimal · negligible · modest | lowly (avoid) | slightly · minimally | `low` adjective; `slightly` adverb |
| fast | rapid · swift · quick | fast / rapidly | rapidly · swiftly · quickly | `rapid growth` / `grew rapidly` |
| slow | gradual · sluggish | slowly | gradually · sluggishly | `gradual decline` |

## 4. The "very" family (intensifiers)

| Type | Synonyms | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Degree | extremely · exceptionally · highly · remarkably · exceedingly · intensely · profoundly · particularly · especially | Modify adjectives/adverbs: `highly likely`, `remarkably similar` |
| Absolute | absolutely · completely · totally · utterly · entirely · wholly · fully | Non-gradable adjectives: `utterly disastrous` |
| Avoid | awfully · terribly · dreadfully · pretty · jolly | Too informal for IELTS writing |

❌ `very unique` · `more better` · `absolutely interesting` (usually)

# PART 3 — BAND 8–9 USAGE RULES

1. **Collocation first.** `a sharp rise` / `rose sharply` ✅; `a sharp rose` ❌.
2. **Repeat-proof.** Never use the same adverb three times in one paragraph — vary it.
3. **Linkers invisible.** Two or three precise ones + referencing is enough.
4. **Accuracy beats ambition.** A wrong "advanced" synonym is an inappropriacy (Band 7 LR).
5. **Don't swap by dictionary.** Synonyms fit one sense only — `big → substantial` works for
   figures, not for `a big decision` (→ `major decision`).
6. **Keep a personal log.** Every time you lose a mark to a wrong word or linker, add it here.
