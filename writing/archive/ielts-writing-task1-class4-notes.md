## 1. What class 4 added

- **Process diagrams:** describe **stages in order**; use **present simple passive**; overview states
  the number of steps and the first and last stages.
- **Maps:** describe **changes over time**; use **past simple passive**; overview names the
  overall transformation and the most striking change.

## 2. Task A — Instant noodle manufacturing process

**Teacher's overview and first body paragraph, upgraded to Band 8–9**

**Introduction**
> The provided diagram illustrates information regarding the instant noodle manufacturing process.

**Overview**
> Overall, it is evident that the production of instant noodles involves eight stages,
> commencing with the sourcing of raw materials and culminating in labelling and sealing.

**Body 1**
> Looking at the details, it can be observed that at the beginning of the process the main
> ingredients are delivered by truck and stored in silos. Next, flour, water and oil are mixed.
> In the subsequent stage, this mixture is transformed into noodle discs using specialised
> equipment.

**Body 2 — completed from the notebook**
> The freshly produced noodle discs are then fried and dried in vegetable oil. Subsequently,
> vegetables and spices are added before the product is packed into cups and sealed, marking
> the final step of the process. Overall, the system is fully mechanised and requires no human
> intervention.

### Grammar focus: passive voice

| Process diagram (general truths) | Map (past changes) |
|---|---|
| **Present simple passive:** `is/are + past participle` | **Past simple passive:** `was/were + past participle` |
| *The ingredients **are delivered** by truck.* | *A new runway **was constructed**.* |
| *The mixture **is transformed** into discs.* | *The farm **was demolished**.* |
| *The product **is packed** and **sealed**.* | *The terminal **was expanded**.* |

**Rule:** if the actor is unimportant or unknown, use the passive. In a process, the *stage* is
the subject, not the worker.

### Corrections to the notebook version

| Notebook wording | Issue | Band 8–9 version |
|---|---|---|
| `there are eight steps for preparing instant noodles` | Vague | `the production of instant noodles involves eight stages` |
| `finishing with labelling and sealing` | Acceptable, but vary | `culminating in labelling and sealing` |
| `the main ingredients of noodle, which is shown, is brought by truck` | Agreement + redundancy | `the main ingredients are delivered by truck` |
| `transformed into disk forms with specialized equipments` | Word form | `transformed into noodle discs using specialised equipment` (equipment is uncountable) |
| `produced noodles disks are fried and dried with vegetable oil` | Word order | `The freshly produced noodle discs are then fried and dried in vegetable oil` |
| `requires no human hands` | Awkward | `requires no human intervention` |
| `spices and [unclear] are added` | Unclear in notebook | `vegetables and spices are added` (confirmed from original diagram) |

## 3. Sequencing linker bank (from the notebook, cleaned)

| Stage | Linkers |
|---|---|
| **Beginning** | First · firstly · at first · initially · to begin with · commencing/starting/beginning/initiating with · at the beginning · in the initial stage · first and foremost |
| **Middle** | secondly · thirdly · later · then · next · after that · followed by · subsequently · in the following step · in the subsequent step |
| **End** | lastly · last · finally · at last · last but not least · culminating/finishing with · ending with |

Note: **step = stage**. Avoid using `firstly / secondly / thirdly` mechanically for all eight
stages — mix time linkers (`next`, `then`, `subsequently`) with stage verbs (`is transformed`,
`is added`).

## 4. Task B — Tilden Airport map (1985–2015)

**Teacher's model, upgraded to Band 8–9**

**Introduction**
> The provided map illustrates information regarding the development of and around Tilden
> Airport over a period of 30 years, between 1985 and 2015.

**Overview**
> Overall, it is evident that the area underwent a number of significant changes. A new runway
> was constructed and the airport was renamed Tilden International Airport. In addition, the
> surrounding land saw a mixture of new development and replacement of older sites.

**Body 1**
> Looking at the details, it can be observed that the international terminal expanded
> considerably during this period, whereas the domestic terminal remained unchanged.
> Furthermore, the car park became only slightly larger, while public transport links improved
> with the addition of a light rail and a bus station at the airport.

**Body 2**
> Upon closer observation, it is apparent that Johnson's Farm was demolished and replaced by a
> business park. In addition, a housing estate was built in the north-east corner of the map.
> Similarly, on the south side, the old factories were demolished and replaced by a hotel and
> conference centre, while Airport Support Services were relocated nearby and enlarged.

### Corrections to the notebook version

| Notebook wording | Issue | Band 8–9 version |
|---|---|---|
| `development at and around Tilden Airport` | Awkward | `the development of and around Tilden Airport` |
| `it has been renamed to Tilden International Airport` | Wrong preposition | `it was renamed Tilden International Airport` |
| `has become bigger` | Informal | `expanded considerably` |
| `the car's park` | Wrong form | `the car park` |
| `because public transport seems to have become more developed` | Speculative | `while public transport links improved` |
| `has been demolished and has become a business park` | Repetition | `was demolished and replaced by a business park` |
| `new factories have been replaced` | Logic | `the old factories were replaced` (assuming the earlier map showed factories) |
| `a large ... for airport support service as well as hotel and conference centre` | Unclear in notebook | `a hotel and conference centre ... alongside the enlarged Airport Support Services` (confirmed from original maps) |
| `The building in direct service has been repositioned` | Awkward | `The building offering direct services was repositioned` |

## 7. Band 8–9 checklist

**Process diagram**
- [ ] Overview states number of stages + first and last stages
- [ ] Stages described in chronological order with varied linkers
- [ ] Present simple passive used accurately (`is delivered`, `are mixed`)
- [ ] No human actor needed; no personal opinion

**Map**
- [ ] Overview names the overall transformation and key changes, no exact figures
- [ ] Past simple passive used accurately (`was constructed`, `were replaced`)
- [ ] Locations used correctly (`in the north-east corner`, `on the south side`)
- [ ] Changes grouped logically (airport buildings, transport, surrounding land)
- [ ] Vocabulary varied from the bank — no repeated `became bigger`

**Both**
- [ ] 165–185 words · 4 paragraphs · no bullets · impersonal register
