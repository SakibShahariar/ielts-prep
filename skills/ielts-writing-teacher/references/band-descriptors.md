# Writing Band Descriptors — condensed official reference

Based on the official IELTS *Writing Band Descriptors* (Updated May 2023) for both
Academic and General Training. Use the exact descriptor wording when justifying a band.
A script must **fully fit the positive features** of a level; bolded/negative features
in the official PDF limit the rating.

Structure for every band: **TA/TR · CC · LR · GRA**.

---

## TASK 1 (Academic and General Training)

### Band 9
- **TA:** All requirements fully and appropriately satisfied; extremely rare lapses in content.
- **CC:** Message followed effortlessly; cohesion very rarely attracts attention; lapses minimal; paragraphing skilfully managed.
- **LR:** Full flexibility and precise use; wide range accurate/appropriate with very natural, sophisticated control; spelling/word-formation errors extremely rare, minimal impact.
- **GRA:** Wide range of structures within the task used with full flexibility and control; punctuation and grammar appropriate throughout; minor errors extremely rare, minimal impact.

### Band 8
- **TA:** Covers all requirements appropriately, relevantly and sufficiently. (A) Key features skilfully selected and clearly presented, highlighted and illustrated. (GT) All bullet points clearly presented and appropriately illustrated or extended. Occasional omissions/lapses possible.
- **CC:** Message followed with ease; information and ideas logically sequenced, cohesion well managed; occasional lapses; paragraphing used sufficiently and appropriately.
- **LR:** Wide resource used fluently and flexibly to convey precise meanings; skilful use of uncommon/idiomatic items where appropriate despite occasional inaccuracies in word choice/collocation; occasional spelling/word-formation errors with minimal impact.
- **GRA:** Wide range of structures flexibly and accurately used; majority of sentences error-free; punctuation well managed; occasional non-systematic errors/inappropriacies with minimal impact.

### Band 7
- **TA:** Covers the requirements; content relevant and accurate with a few omissions/lapses; format appropriate. (A) Key features covered and clearly highlighted but could be more fully/appropriately illustrated or extended; **clear overview**, data categorised, main trends/differences identified. (GT) All bullet points covered and clearly highlighted but could be extended; clear purpose; tone consistent and appropriate; lapses minimal.
- **CC:** Information and ideas logically organised with clear progression throughout; a few lapses; range of cohesive devices including reference and substitution used flexibly but with some inaccuracies or over/under use.
- **LR:** Sufficient to allow some flexibility and precision; some ability to use less common/idiomatic items; awareness of style and collocation evident though inappropriacies occur; only a few spelling/word-formation errors, not detracting from overall clarity.
- **GRA:** Variety of complex structures used with some flexibility and accuracy; grammar and punctuation generally well controlled; error-free sentences frequent; a few errors may persist but do not impede communication.

### Band 6
- **TA:** Focuses on the requirements; appropriate format. (A) Key features covered and adequately highlighted; **a relevant overview is attempted**; information selected and supported using figures/data. (GT) All bullet points covered and adequately highlighted; purpose generally clear; minor tone inconsistencies. Some irrelevant/inappropriate/inaccurate information in areas of detail; some details missing or excessive; further extension needed.
- **CC:** Information and ideas generally arranged coherently with clear overall progression; cohesive devices used to some good effect but cohesion within/between sentences may be faulty or mechanical (misuse, overuse, omission); reference and substitution may lack flexibility or clarity, causing repetition or error.
- **LR:** Generally adequate and appropriate; meaning generally clear despite rather restricted range or lack of precision; risk-takers may use wider range with higher inaccuracy; some spelling/word-formation errors but these do not impede communication.
- **GRA:** Mix of simple and complex forms but flexibility limited; complex structures not as accurate as simple ones; errors in grammar and punctuation occur but rarely impede communication.

### Band 5
- **TA:** Generally addresses the requirements; format may be inappropriate. (A) Key features not adequately covered; recounting of detail mainly mechanical; may be no data. (GT) Bullets presented but one or more not adequately covered; purpose unclear at times; tone variable/inappropriate. Tendency to focus on details without the bigger picture; irrelevant/inappropriate/inaccurate material in key areas; limited detail when extending.
- **CC:** Organisation evident but not wholly logical with possible lack of overall progression; underlying coherence; relationships follow but sentences not fluently linked; limited/overuse of cohesive devices with inaccuracy; writing repetitive due to inadequate reference/substitution.
- **LR:** Limited but minimally adequate; simple vocabulary used accurately but little variation; frequent lapses in appropriacy, simplifications/repetitions; spelling/word-formation errors noticeable and may cause some difficulty for the reader.
- **GRA:** Range limited and repetitive; complex sentences attempted but tend to be faulty; greatest accuracy in simple sentences; frequent grammatical errors may cause difficulty; punctuation may be faulty.

### Band 4
- **TA:** Attempt to address the task. (A) Few key features selected. (GT) Not all bullet points presented; purpose not clearly explained/may be confused; tone inappropriate. Format may be inappropriate; presented features/bullets may be irrelevant, repetitive, inaccurate or inappropriate.
- **CC:** Information/ideas evident but not arranged coherently, no clear progression; relationships unclear or inadequately marked; basic cohesive devices inaccurate or repetitive; inaccurate use or lack of substitution/reference.
- **LR:** Limited, inadequate or unrelated to the task; basic, repetitive vocabulary; inappropriate lexical chunks (memorised phrases, formulaic language, input language); word choice/word-formation/spelling errors may impede meaning.
- **GRA:** Very limited range of structures; subordinate clauses rare, simple sentences predominate; some structures accurate but frequent grammatical errors may impede meaning; punctuation often faulty or inadequate.

### Band 3
- **TA:** Does not address the requirements (possibly through misunderstanding); features/bullets largely irrelevant; limited information, used repetitively.
- **CC:** No apparent logical organisation; ideas discernible but difficult to relate; minimal sequencers/cohesive devices not indicating logical relations; difficulty identifying referencing.
- **LR:** Inadequate (may be due to underlength); possible over-dependence on input or memorised language; control of word choice/spelling very limited, errors predominate and may severely impede meaning.
- **GRA:** Sentence forms attempted but errors in grammar and punctuation predominate (except memorised/input phrases), preventing most meaning from coming through; length may be insufficient to show sentence control.

### Band 2
- **TA:** Content barely relates to the task.
- **CC:** Little relevant message, or entire response may be off-topic; little evidence of control of organisational features.
- **LR:** Extremely limited, few recognisable strings apart from memorised phrases; no apparent control of word formation/spelling.
- **GRA:** Little or no evidence of sentence forms except memorised phrases.

### Band 1
- Responses of **20 words or fewer** are rated Band 1 in every criterion. Content wholly unrelated; copied rubric discounted; writing fails to communicate any message (virtual non-writer); no resource beyond isolated words; no rateable language.

### Band 0
- Only where the candidate did not attend/attempt, used a language other than English throughout, or there is proof the answer was totally memorised.

## TASK 2 (Academic and General Training)

### Band 9
- **TR:** Prompt appropriately addressed and **explored in depth**; clear, fully developed position directly answering the question/s; ideas relevant, fully extended and well supported; lapses in content/support extremely rare.
- **CC:** Message followed effortlessly; cohesion very rarely attracts attention; lapses minimal; paragraphing skilfully managed.
- **LR:** Full flexibility and precise use widely evident; wide range accurate/appropriate with very natural, sophisticated control; spelling/word-formation errors extremely rare, minimal impact.
- **GRA:** Wide range of structures with full flexibility and control; punctuation and grammar appropriate throughout; minor errors extremely rare, minimal impact.

### Band 8
- **TR:** Prompt appropriately and sufficiently addressed; clear, well-developed position; ideas relevant, well extended and supported; occasional omissions/lapses in content.
- **CC:** Message followed with ease; ideas logically sequenced, cohesion well managed; occasional lapses; paragraphing used sufficiently and appropriately.
- **LR:** Wide resource used fluently and flexibly to convey precise meanings; skilful uncommon/idiomatic items despite occasional word choice/collocation inaccuracies; occasional spelling/word-formation errors with minimal impact.
- **GRA:** Wide range of structures flexibly and accurately used; majority of sentences error-free; punctuation well managed; occasional non-systematic errors/inappropriacies with minimal impact.

### Band 7
- **TR:** Main parts of the prompt appropriately addressed; clear, developed position; main ideas extended and supported but may over-generalise or lack focus/precision in supporting material.
- **CC:** Information and ideas logically organised with clear progression throughout (minor lapses); range of cohesive devices including reference/substitution used flexibly but with some inaccuracies or over/under use; paragraphing generally used effectively, sequencing within paragraphs generally logical.
- **LR:** Sufficient to allow some flexibility and precision; some less common/idiomatic items; awareness of style and collocation evident though inappropriacies occur; only a few spelling/word-formation errors, not detracting from clarity.
- **GRA:** Variety of complex structures with some flexibility and accuracy; grammar and punctuation generally well controlled; error-free sentences frequent; a few errors may persist but do not impede communication.

### Band 6
- **TR:** Main parts addressed (some more fully than others); appropriate format; position directly relevant but conclusions may be unclear, unjustified or repetitive; main ideas relevant but insufficiently developed/unclear, with some less relevant or inadequate support.
- **CC:** Generally coherent with clear overall progression; cohesive devices to some good effect but cohesion may be faulty or mechanical; reference/substitution may lack flexibility or clarity, causing repetition/error; paragraphing may not always be logical and central topic not always clear.
- **LR:** Generally adequate and appropriate; meaning generally clear despite restricted range or lack of precision; risk-takers use wider range with higher inaccuracy; some spelling/word-formation errors but not impeding communication.
- **GRA:** Mix of simple and complex forms, flexibility limited; complex structures less accurate than simple ones; grammar/punctuation errors occur but rarely impede communication.

### Band 5
- **TR:** Main parts incompletely addressed; format may be inappropriate; position expressed but development unclear; some main ideas limited, insufficiently developed or irrelevant; possible repetition.
- **CC:** Organisation evident but not wholly logical, possible lack of overall progression; ideas follow but sentences not fluently linked; limited/overuse of cohesive devices with inaccuracy; writing repetitive; paragraphing inadequate or missing.
- **LR:** Limited but minimally adequate; simple vocabulary accurate but little variation; frequent lapses in appropriacy, simplifications/repetitions; spelling/word-formation errors noticeable and may cause difficulty.
- **GRA:** Range limited and repetitive; complex sentences attempted but faulty; greatest accuracy in simple sentences; frequent grammatical errors cause some difficulty; punctuation may be faulty.

### Band 4
- **TR:** Prompt tackled minimally or tangentially, possibly through misunderstanding; format may be inappropriate; position discernible but reader must read carefully; main ideas difficult to identify and lacking relevance, clarity or support; large parts repetitive.
- **CC:** Ideas evident but not arranged coherently, no clear progression; relationships unclear/inadequately marked; basic cohesive devices inaccurate/repetitive; inaccurate or absent referencing; may be no paragraphing and no clear main topic.
- **LR:** Limited, inadequate or unrelated; basic repetitive vocabulary; inappropriate lexical chunks (memorised/formulaic/input language); word choice/word-formation/spelling errors may impede meaning.
- **GRA:** Very limited range; subordinate clauses rare, simple sentences predominate; some accurate structures but frequent errors may impede meaning; punctuation often faulty or inadequate.

### Band 3
- **TR:** No part adequately addressed or prompt misunderstood; no relevant position identified, little direct response; few ideas, irrelevant or insufficiently developed; content barely related.
- **CC:** No apparent logical organisation; minimal sequencers/cohesive devices not indicating logical relations; difficulty identifying referencing; paragraphing attempts unhelpful; little relevant message or entirely off-topic.
- **LR:** Inadequate (may be underlength); over-dependence on input/memorised language; control of word choice/spelling very limited, errors predominate and may severely impede meaning.
- **GRA:** Sentence forms attempted but grammar and punctuation errors predominate (except memorised phrases), preventing most meaning; length may be insufficient to show sentence control.

### Band 2
- **TR:** No position identifiable; glimpses of one or two ideas without development.
- **CC:** Little relevant message or off-topic; little evidence of control of organisational features.
- **LR:** Extremely limited, few recognisable strings apart from memorised phrases; no apparent control of word formation/spelling.
- **GRA:** Little or no evidence of sentence forms except memorised phrases.

### Band 1
- Responses of **20 words or fewer** → Band 1 in every criterion. Content wholly unrelated; copied rubric discounted; fails to communicate; only isolated words; no rateable language.

### Band 0
- Not attended/attempted, another language throughout, or proof of totally memorised answer.
