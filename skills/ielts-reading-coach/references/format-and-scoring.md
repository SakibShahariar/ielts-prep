# IELTS Reading: Format, Scoring and Answer Rules

Source base: official IELTS partners (ielts.org), IDP IELTS, IELTS Liz, and other
IELTS preparation references. Verified November 2026.

## 1. Format

| | **Academic** | **General Training** |
|---|---|---|
| Time | 60 minutes | 60 minutes |
| Texts | 3 passages, 2,150–2,750 words total; books, journals, magazines, newspapers, online; general-interest undergraduate/postgraduate topics; at least one contains detailed logical argument; may include diagrams/graphs | 3 sections, 2,150–2,375 words total; increasing difficulty — Section 1 everyday/social texts (notices, ads, timetables), Section 2 workplace texts (job descriptions, contracts, training), Section 3 one longer general-interest text |
| Questions | 40 | 40 |
| Marking | 1 mark per correct answer; no negative marking | same |

- Sources for Academic: books, journals, magazines, newspapers, online resources for a
  non-specialist audience. Technical vocabulary is glossed if used.
- Question types are essentially the same for Academic and GT; the techniques are the
  same. Only the texts differ (IELTS Liz).
- From mid-2026 IELTS is computer-delivered only; in the Reading test the computer
  shows a clock and offers a text-highlighting tool. There is **no extra transfer time**
  in Reading (unlike paper-based Listening).

## 2. Official question types

Academic lists 11 official types; GT lists 8. Practitioners often count 14 because
"completion" has several formats and T/F/NG and Y/N/NG are counted separately.

**Academic (11):** multiple choice · identifying information (True/False/Not Given) ·
identifying writer's views/claims (Yes/No/Not Given) · matching information · matching
headings · matching features · matching sentence endings · sentence completion ·
summary/note/table/flow-chart completion · diagram label completion · short-answer questions.

**General Training (8):** multiple choice · identifying information (T/F/NG) · matching
information · matching headings · matching features · summary/note/sentence/table/
flow-chart completion · diagram label completion · short-answer questions.

GT does not normally include Yes/No/Not Given, matching sentence endings or list selection.

## 3. Raw-score → band conversion

Each correct answer = 1 mark, out of 40. IELTS publishes **average** marks needed at
each band; precise cut-offs vary slightly between test versions.

| Band | Academic Reading (avg. correct) | GT Reading (avg. correct) |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 15 | 4 |
| 6 | 23 | 5 |
| 7 | 30 | 6 |
| 8 | 35 | 7 |

Notes:
- Academic and GT Reading use the same scale, but GT usually requires **more** correct
  answers for a given band because its texts are easier.
- Band 9 is typically ~39–40 correct, but IELTS does not publish a fixed published
  average for it here — treat it as approximate.
- Bands are reported in whole and half bands (e.g. 6.5).

## 4. Answer-form rules

- **No negative marking.** A wrong answer simply earns no point — always guess.
- Answers are **100% correct or wrong**; no partial credit.
- **Spelling and grammar count** in Reading (as in Listening). A wrong plural or
  misspelling loses the mark. Words in brackets in an answer key are optional.
- Respect the **word limit** (e.g. "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS"). Exceeding it is wrong.
- **Capitalisation does not matter** — `TRUE`, `True` and `true` are all accepted.
  Abbreviations are accepted: `T/F/NG` for True/False/Not Given, `Y/N/NG` for
  Yes/No/Not Given.
- **Never mix the systems.** `True` instead of `Yes`, or `Y` instead of `T`, is wrong.
- Commas are unnecessary in multi-word answers. Brackets (e.g. `pen(s)`) are not
  allowed from candidates.
- Answers can be changed any time during the 60 minutes. There is no extra check time.
- For the computer test, use the highlight tool to mark keywords in the passage.

## 5. 2026 context

- Paper-based IELTS is being phased out from mid-2026; all tests on computer, with
  "Writing on Paper" available in selected markets only for the Writing component.
- Reading scoring and interpretation are unchanged; TRFs updated from 15 July 2026
  with Ofqual branding only. Scores remain valid for the recommended two years.
